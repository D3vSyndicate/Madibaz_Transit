# Madiba Transit — Intelligent Shuttle Queue & Fleet Management System

## 📌 Project Overview

**Madiba Transit** is an intelligent campus transportation management platform developed for Nelson Mandela University (NMU). Created by **D3vSyndicate**, the system is designed to digitize and optimize the university's shuttle operations across its campus network and off-campus residence hubs.

---

## ❓ The Problem & Why It Was Created

Students relying on the NMU shuttle network face daily operational bottlenecks that disrupt their academic routines:

* **Long Queues & Overcrowding:** Peak departure times (especially morning shifts and mid-day/afternoon slots like 14:30 and 17:30) lead to severe congestion at trackside loading bays.
* **Transportation Uncertainty:** Commuters lack real-time visibility into shuttle arrival times, seat availability, or their position in line, forcing many to arrive unnecessarily early or wait over 30 minutes.
* **Unfair Boarding Practices:** Physical queues often suffer from queue-cutting, seat hoarding for friends, and boarding confusion.
* **Operational Blind Spots:** Shuttle managers and university administrators have limited real-time visibility into passenger demand, third-party fleet status, or service disruptions like vehicle breakdowns.

---

## 💡 The Solution

Madiba Transit introduces a centralized, multi-role digital ecosystem to streamline shuttle operations from end to end:

* **Virtual Queueing & Reservations:** Students can view schedules, check seat availability in real time, reserve seats online, and join virtual queues when capacity is reached, eliminating physical overcrowding and uncertainty.
* **Digital Boarding Pass Verification:** Trackside marshals use digital verification tools to quickly validate student reservations at loading bays, ensuring fair and orderly boarding while preventing double-boarding.
* **Fleet & Driver Management:** Drivers can view assigned routes, update trip statuses, and log operational incidents (like delays or breakdowns). External shuttle managers can monitor fleet performance and reassign drivers dynamically during disruptions.
* **Administrative Oversight:** Centralized analytics give university management clear visibility into route demand, peak utilization, and student feedback to support long-term transport planning.

## 📋 Core Features by Module

* **Student Module:** Interactive timetables, real-time shuttle tracking, online seat bookings, dynamic boarding passes, and virtual queueing.
* **Marshal Module:** Digital passenger verification, real-time capacity tracking, and queue management at loading bays.
* **Driver Module:** Shift schedules, trip progress tracking, and breakdown/delay incident logging.
* **Shuttle Manager Module:** Fleet tracking, driver dispatching, route assignment, and operational disruption management.
* **Admin Module:** User role management, system settings, feedback reviews, and aggregate route usage analytics.
